## Step 56 — RLOR's one-hop is real, and it is a P_succ floor tie

Seed 20260921, N=40, v=2.0 m/s, azimuth 5.968 rad (same placement as
Steps 49/52/53). Snapshot of `RlorInspiredProtocol` at packet times
t=0, 300, 600 s on the rigid Scenario II translation. Not a new
traffic experiment. Raw: `results/STEP56_RLOR_PSUCC.md`.

| t (s) | sink in I | empty I | nextHop=sink | nextHop=AUV | nextHop=none | \|I\| (shown) | P_succ(sink) > P_succ(best AUV) | P_succ tie |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|---:|---:|
| 0 | **40/40** | 0 | 40 | 0 | 0 | 31–40 | 0 | **40** |
| 300 | 40/40 | 0 | 40 | 0 | 0 | 31–40 | 0 | 40 |
| 600 | 39/40 | 0 | 39 | 1 | 0 | 31–40 | 0 | 39 |

**Not a degenerate fallback.** Every source has a large I_RLOR
(N_range ∩ N_SNR). Empty-I → nextHop = −1 → VOID; that never
happens. The candidate logic from Steps 13/26 is being exercised.

**Not “the sink has strictly higher P_succ.”** For every source at
t=0, `P_succ(i, sink) = P_succ(i, best AUV) = 0.9999996`, which is
exactly the SPEC 1.3 BER-floor plateau `(1 − 10⁻⁹)⁴⁰⁰`. January
SNR to the 50 m sink and to nearby AUVs both sit well above the
floor (typical 20–50 dB). Greedy argmax cannot tell them apart.
SPEC 3.3 then breaks ties by smallest id, and the sink is id 0.

Representative t=0 (source, P_succ, SNR):

| src | P_succ(sink) | SNR(sink) | best AUV | P_succ(AUV) | SNR(AUV) | why sink |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| 1 | 0.9999996 | **46.3 dB** | 2 | 0.9999996 | 29.7 dB | SNR and id |
| 5 | 0.9999996 | 32.1 dB | 1 | 0.9999996 | **41.8 dB** | **id only** (AUV is louder) |
| 6 | 0.9999996 | 19.9 dB | 1 | 0.9999996 | **23.7 dB** | id only |

At t=0, 23/40 ties also have SNR_sink ≥ SNR_alt; **17/40 have a
strictly louder AUV** and still pick the sink. At t=600 one source
drops the sink from I (range/SNR after 1200 m) and RLOR *does*
pick an AUV — so the hop is not hard-wired to node 0.

**Paper sentence:** RLOR-inspired is one-hop because high-SNR
January links saturate `P_succ` at the BER floor, and the sink
wins the id tie. It is not an empty-set fallback and not a claim
that the sink is uniquely the best acoustic neighbor. The 1.000 ±
0.001 hop count across the 25 Scenario II runs is this mechanism
plus `--boundary=remove` taking leavers out before a two-hop is
needed.

## Step 57 — proposed eddy formula and kinematics-only check

### Formula (SPEC Part 9)

One counterclockwise Rankine-capped eddy at the volume XY centroid.
Horizontal only.

```
r = ||(x,y) − (3000, 3000)||
ê_θ = (−(y−3000), x−3000) / r        # 0 at r = 0
v_θ(r) = V_eddy · min(r / R_core, 1)
v_eddy_mean = (v_θ ê_θx, v_θ ê_θy, 0)

V_eddy = 2.0 m/s     # Scenario II midpoint; |v| stays in [0.5, 3.5]
R_core = 2000 m
```

Independent per-AUV Gauss-Markov (`gaussMarkovStep`, ζ = 0.95):

```
v^i(t+dt) = ζ v^i(t) + (1−ζ) v_eddy_mean(p_i)
            + sqrt(1−ζ²) σ ⊙ w^i(t)

ζ = 0.95, dt = 1 s, σ = (1, 1, 0) m/s, w^i ~ N(0, I)
v^i(0) = v_eddy_mean(p_i(0))
sink velocity = 0, v_cmd = 0
```

**FLAG:** σ_z = 0. A 1 m/s vertical innovation exits the 500 m
column on a 600 s window (same reason Scenario II forbade 3-D
isotropic drift). The generic stepper still accepts a nonzero
σ_z if a later experiment sets it.

Two AUVs on opposite sides of the centre have mean |v_rel| = 2V
at the cap (unit test 22e). Scenario II this is identically 0.

`--scenario=iii` without `--diagKinematics` is a hard error.
Traffic is not wired.
