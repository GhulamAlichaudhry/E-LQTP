## Step 58 — σ_z=0 is the permanent Scenario III design

Horizontal-only eddy motion is the intended permanent behaviour, not
a placeholder and not a `--diagKinematics`-only safety rail.

Mid-latitude mesoscale / submesoscale eddies at these length and
depth scales are geostrophic: the dominant flow is horizontal. A
1 m/s vertical Gauss-Markov innovation would also empty the 500 m
column in a few hundred seconds (the same FLAG that forbade 3-D
isotropic drift in Scenario II). Vertical position stays at the
placement \(z\).

Documented in SPEC Part 9 alongside the Rankine formula. The generic
stepper still accepts a nonzero \(\sigma_z\) if a later, explicitly
justified experiment sets it; Scenario III does not.

Compiled default: `GaussMarkovConfig::sigma_z = 0`.

## Step 59 — radial vs tangential \(v_{\mathrm{rel}}\)

Same kinematics-only setup as Step 57: seed 20260921, \(N=40\),
\(T=120\) s, no packets. Placement: 13 AUVs inside \(R_{\mathrm{core}}\),
27 outside. \(n=94380\) AUV–AUV pair-ticks (\(C(40,2)\times 121\)).

Radial = \(|(v_b-v_a)\cdot\hat{r}|\) (range-rate; the only component
\(H_k\) / SNR can see, Steps 35–38). Tangential = the remainder
(unobservable).

Two passes: **A** is the documented Scenario III process
(\(\sigma_{xy}=1\), \(\sigma_z=0\)). **B** is mean-flow only
(\(\sigma=0\)) so the Rankine geometry is visible on its own.

### Finding — proceed to Step 60

The process the trigger actually sees is Pass A, not Pass B.
\(\sigma_{xy}=1\) is part of the documented Scenario III design
(SPEC Part 9), not an extra. Under that process:

- \(\|v_{\mathrm{rad}}\|\) mean **1.06 m/s** vs
  \(\|v_{\mathrm{tan}}\|\) **2.54 m/s** — same order of magnitude
  (ratio 0.42), not orders of magnitude smaller.
- This is not Scenario II in a new disguise. Scenario II had
  AUV–AUV \(v_{\mathrm{rel}}\) identically 0. Here the EKF’s
  observable channel (range-rate) has a genuine, time-varying
  \(O(1)\) m/s signal on most pairs.

The Rankine field still does the job Scenario II could not: two
AUVs at different bearings have different \(v_{\mathrm{eddy,mean}}\),
so they are not in uniform lockstep. The GM innovation supplies
most of the radial projection the trigger needs. No extra
convergent/divergent term and no re-placement at varying radii
are required before the multi-seed comparison.
