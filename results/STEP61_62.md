## Step 61 — how long does a closing neighbor stay closing?

Same kinematics-only setup as Steps 57/59: seed 20260921, \(N=40\),
\(T=120\) s, full Gauss-Markov \(\sigma_{xy}=1\), \(\sigma_z=0\).
Signed \(v_{\mathrm{rad}}=(v_b-v_a)\cdot\hat{r}\), SPEC 2.1
convention (negative = closing). 780 AUV–AUV pairs × 121 ticks.

Same-sign run = consecutive 1 s ticks with the same nonzero sign.
A zero or a flip ends the run.

| | mean | median | p25 | p75 | min | max |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| same-sign run (s) | **8.63** | **3.0** | **1.0** | 10.0 | 1 | 121 |

- 10 923 runs (5 404 closing, 5 519 opening).
- Ticks are 49.0% closing / 50.9% opening / 0.08% zero — no
  preferred radial direction, as a rotation should look.
- **10 143 sign flips.** 779 / 780 pairs flip at least once in 120 s
  (~13 flips per pair).
- Sign autocorrelation: ACF(1)=0.78, ACF(5)=0.54, ACF(10)=0.38,
  ACF(20)=0.22. \(\tau_{1/e}=10.4\) s.

Pair (1,2) is a heavy-tail example, not the median: it sits closing
for ~20 s, flips, then holds opening from \(t\approx 50\) through
120 s. The distribution is a short-run bulk (median 3 s, a quarter
of runs last 1 s) plus a long tail.

### Inter-hop decision interval

A routing decision is made in `Forward()` once at generation and
once at every hop arrival (`learnFrom` + `nextHop` on the current
candidate set). The packet then flies

```
t_flight = airtime + range / c = 0.4 s + d / 1500
```

and the next decision for that packet is at arrival.

Measured on the Step 62 LQARP / E-LQTP runs (same eddy, \(N=40\),
\(T_{\mathrm{sim}}=600\)):

| | hop flight (s) | \(T_{\mathrm{e2e}}/\mathrm{hops}\) (s) |
|---|---:|---:|
| LQARP | **2.030 ± 0.102** | 4.146 / 2.046 = 2.03 |
| E-LQTP \(\beta_1=0\) (all \(\beta_2\)) | 2.03–2.04 | ~2.03 |

Implied mean hop length \((2.03-0.4)\times 1500\approx 2445\) m.
A typical successful path is two hops, ~4 s door-to-door. Step 60’s
\(\beta_1=\beta_2=1\) E-LQTP was 3.25 hops / 6.96 s, still ~2.1 s
per hop — same flight, more hops.

### Persistence vs the decision clock

| timescale | seconds |
|---|---:|
| median same-sign run | **3.0** |
| p25 run | **1.0** |
| mean run | 8.6 |
| sign ACF \(1/e\) | 10.4 |
| one hop (decision → next decision) | **2.03** |
| typical LQARP path (\(T_{\mathrm{e2e}}\)) | **4.15** |
| Step 60 E-LQTP path | 6.96 |

The median closing/opening spell is the same order as one hop and
shorter than a multi-hop transit. A quarter of spells die in a
single second — before the packet they just ranked even arrives.
That is exactly the condition under which
\(c_{\mathrm{drift}}=c_{\mathrm{del}}(1+\beta_2\sigma(-v_{\mathrm{rel}}))\)
rewards a neighbor that will not still be closing at the next hop.

GM velocity itself has \(\tau\approx -1/\ln 0.95\approx 20\) s; the
*sign* of the radial projection flips faster because \(\hat{r}\)
rotates with the pair. Pair (1,2)’s Step 59 \(|v_{\mathrm{rad}}|\)
swing 0 → 5 m/s is the same series: the sign at \(t=10\) is closing,
at \(t=30\) opening, at \(t=40\) closing, at \(t=50\) opening.

**Step 61 supports the mechanism of the hypothesis.** The eddy does
not give \(c_{\mathrm{drift}}\) a persistent closing trend on the
timescale of a routing decision.

## Step 62 — isolate \(c_{\mathrm{drift}}\): \(\beta_1=0\), sweep \(\beta_2\)

LQARP vs E-LQTP only. Same 5 seeds, \(N=40\), \(T_{\mathrm{sim}}=600\) s,
Scenario III eddy. \(\beta_1=0\) so \(c_{\mathrm{pred}}=c_{\mathrm{lin}}\)
(LQARP’s factor). \(\beta_2\in\{0,0.25,0.5,1.0,2.0\}\). E-LQTP still
has the event trigger (\(\varepsilon_0=2\), \(s_{\mathrm{cap}}=2\)).
30 runs, all `metrics_finite=1`, VOID=0.

E-LQTP first-fire is **0.708 ± 0.059** at every \(\beta_2\) (same
trigger; \(\beta_2\) is not in \(e_k\)). \(O_{\mathrm{ctrl}}\) is

Hops / \(T_{\mathrm{e2e}}\) / \(E_{\mathrm{bit}}\) relative to LQARP:

| \(\beta_2\) | hops | \(T_{\mathrm{e2e}}\) | \(E_{\mathrm{bit}}\) |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0 | **1.002×** | 1.005× | 1.002× |
| 0.25 | **0.998×** | 0.999× | 0.998× |
| 0.5 | 1.015× | 1.016× | 1.015× |
| 1.0 | **1.056×** | 1.062× | 1.056× |
| 2.0 | 1.017× | 1.018× | 1.016× |

### What the sweep says about the hypothesis

**Not monotonic.** \(\beta_2=1\) is the worst of the five
(+0.115 hops, +0.26 s). \(\beta_2=2\) comes back down. This is
not “any \(\beta_2>0\) only adds cost” in a clean dose-response.

**No intermediate \(\beta_2\) recovers a routing advantage.**
The best E-LQTP points (\(\beta_2=0\) and \(0.25\)) are a
statistical tie with LQARP on hops / delay / energy. Drift-awareness
does not help on this eddy. It also does not, by itself, reproduce
Step 60.

**\(\beta_2=0\) is as good as it gets for \(c_{\mathrm{drift}}\),**
and it is LQARP’s data plane plus the event-trigger
\(O_{\mathrm{ctrl}}\). The trigger / \(N_{\mathrm{stab}}\) path is
**not** what inflated hops: \(\beta_1=\beta_2=0\) is 2.050 hops
against LQARP’s 2.046.

### The Step 60 blowup is \(\beta_1\), not \(\beta_2\)

| E-LQTP config | hops | \(T_{\mathrm{e2e}}\) | vs LQARP hops |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Step 62 \(\beta_1=0\), \(\beta_2=1\) | 2.161 | 4.405 | **1.06×** |
| Step 60 \(\beta_1=1\), \(\beta_2=1\) | 3.250 | 6.961 | **1.59×** |

Same eddy, same seeds, same trigger. Turning \(\beta_1\) from 0 to 1
is the difference between “a few percent” and “sixty percent more
hops.” \(c_{\mathrm{pred}}=c_{\mathrm{lin}}+\beta_1\max(0,e_k-e_{k-1})\)
is what the Step 60 comparison actually stressed. That is *not*
the noise-chasing-\(c_{\mathrm{drift}}\) story, and it is *not*
validated: \(\beta_1\) has never been swept on Scenario III
(this step was asked not to).

## How to write Scenario III

Neither of the two pre-written endings is the whole truth.
The honest one is both:

**On \(c_{\mathrm{drift}}\) (this sweep):** the design assumption
of persistent relative motion does not hold in this rotational
field (Step 61: median sign hold 3 s vs hop 2.0 s). No \(\beta_2\)
produces a routing win over LQARP. \(\beta_2=0\) (i.e. \(c_{\mathrm{del}}\))
is the best setting; \(\beta_2=1\) is a small, non-monotonic
penalty, not a disaster. That is a real, citable statement about
*when* drift-awareness helps: it needs a closing trend that lasts
longer than a hop. A uniform current (Scenario II’s AUV–sink
geometry) can do that. This eddy’s radial sign cannot.

**On Step 60’s headline cost:** do not blame \(\beta_2=1\) /
\(c_{\mathrm{drift}}\) for the 1.59× hop ratio. That ratio is a
\(\beta_1=1\) effect. \(\beta_1=\beta_2=1\) was never a validated
operating point; it was the first non-identity pair carried through
every diagnostic. A paper that quotes Step 60 as “E-LQTP vs LQARP
under eddy drift” without a \(\beta_1\) sweep would be quoting an
unswept \(c_{\mathrm{pred}}\) weight, not a failed drift term.

The remaining diagnostic, when wanted: the same 5-seed LQARP vs
E-LQTP grid at \(\beta_2=0\) and swept \(\beta_1\). Not started
here.

## Checkpoint

- Radial sign is short-lived (median 3 s). The \(c_{\mathrm{drift}}\)
  time-horizon assumption fails on this eddy.
- Isolating \(\beta_2\) at \(\beta_1=0\): no routing advantage at
  any value; \(\beta_2=0\) ties LQARP; \(\beta_2=1\) is +6% hops,
  not +59%.
- Step 60’s large cost is \(\beta_1\), still unswept.
- Unit suite PASS=40 FAIL=0.
