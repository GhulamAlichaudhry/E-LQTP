## Step 68 — SPEC.md

Three permanent additions; the \(\beta=1/1\) history is kept, not
deleted.

**Part 2.6 addendum.** The additive form
\(c_{\mathrm{pred}}=c_{\mathrm{lin}}+\beta_1\max(0,e_k-e_{k-1})\)
is scale-inconsistent on the BER-floor plateau
(\(c_{\mathrm{lin}}\approx 4\times 10^{-7}\) for \(l=400\), Part
1.3). \(O(1)\) \(\beta_1\) overrides \(c_{\mathrm{lin}}\). A
blend-scale \(\beta_1\) (\(10^{-5}\)–\(10^{-6}\)) has no favorable
effect either (Step 66). The one-step Kalman trend has p25
persistence 1 s. \(\beta_1=0\) is the validated default.

**Part 2.7 addendum.** \(c_{\mathrm{drift}}\) has no scale bug.
Scenario III cost is small and non-monotonic (Step 62:
0.998×–1.056× hops; no improvement). The design assumption
(persistent closing/opening \(v_{\mathrm{rel}}\)) does not hold at
eddy-rotation timescales (Step 61: median 3 s, p25 1 s). The
benefit is scenario-dependent. \(\beta_2=0\) is the validated
default for Scenario III; a Scenario II-specific non-zero
\(\beta_2\) sweep remains open.

**Known Limitations and Future Work.** Consolidates both findings
and records a relative-form / longer-memory \(c_{\mathrm{pred}}\)
as future work, not implemented here.

Also updated so future runs cannot silently pick up the
placeholder: Section 4.1 table, Part 4 (periodic shares the 0/0
default), Part 6 inventory, Part 8 CLI, Scenario III wiring,
`BuildProtocol` NaN default \(1.0\to 0.0\), and the module README.

## What the paper can now say

E-LQTP’s contribution, at the validated operating point, is the
event-triggered discovery layer:

- Scenario II: data plane = LQARP; \(O_{\mathrm{ctrl}}=0.33\times\)
  Fixed-Periodic at \(v=2\) (0.05×–0.62× across 0.5–3.5 m/s);
  first-fire 15%–90% with drift.
- Scenario III: data plane = LQARP (1.002× hops); \(O_{\mathrm{ctrl}}=0.51\times\)
  Fixed-Periodic; first-fire 0.71.

The drift-aware reward terms, as currently formulated, are a
documented negative result with a precise mechanism (Part 2.6
scale mismatch + Part 2.7 persistence mismatch), not a silent
omission. \(\beta_1=\beta_2=1\) tables remain in the repo as the
diagnostic record.

A Scenario II-specific \(\beta_2\) sweep, and a relative-form
\(c_{\mathrm{pred}}\), are SPEC future work.
